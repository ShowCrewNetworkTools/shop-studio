Shop Studio brings your listings, sales data, and buyer communication into one focused workspace — built around the way you actually work.

What it does

Everything your shop needs, nothing it doesn't.

01

Listing Management

Create, edit and publish product listings directly to your Etsy shop. Keep your entire catalogue organised, consistent and up to date without switching tabs.

02

Titles & Tags

Write and refine listing titles, tags and descriptions with a clear, distraction-free editor. Make your products easier to find by the buyers who matter.

03

Sales Tracking

Read your shop's sales data to understand what's performing and what isn't. Make informed decisions about your product range based on your own numbers.

04

Buyer Messages

Read and respond to buyer messages about their orders without leaving your workflow. Stay on top of customer communication without losing focus.

Privacy & Security

Your shop data stays yours.

Shop Studio connects to your Etsy shop via OAuth — the same secure standard used across the web. Your credentials and shop data are never stored externally or shared with any third party.

  • Connects via OAuth — your Etsy password is never stored
  • Used solely for managing your own shop
  • No data sold, shared, or transferred to third parties
  • Not distributed to any other sellers
