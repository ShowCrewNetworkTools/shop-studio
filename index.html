Shop Studio is a private tool I built to manage the Peanut Parade Etsy shop — keeping listings, sales and buyer messages organised in one quiet workspace.

What it does

Built around the shop. Built for the shop.

Listing Management

Create, edit and publish product listings directly to the Peanut Parade shop. Keep the catalogue consistent, organised and always up to date.

Titles & Tags

Write and refine listing titles, tags and descriptions so the right buyers find the right products at the right moment.

Sales Tracking

Read shop sales data to understand what's connecting with customers and make thoughtful decisions about the range.

Buyer Messages

Read and respond to buyer messages about their orders — keeping every customer moment as warm as the products themselves.

Privacy & Security

Your shop data is always safe.

Shop Studio connects to Etsy via OAuth. It is used solely by me to manage the Peanut Parade shop. No shop data is stored externally or shared with any third party.

  • Connects via OAuth — Etsy password never stored
  • Used solely for the Peanut Parade shop
  • No data sold, shared or transferred externally
  • Not distributed to any other sellers
